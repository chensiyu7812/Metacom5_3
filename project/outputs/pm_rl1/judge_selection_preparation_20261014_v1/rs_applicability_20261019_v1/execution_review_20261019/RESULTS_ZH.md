## 最关键的一组原文

当前用户只说：**“Honestly, things have been a bit tough lately.”**（说实话，最近有些难。）合法历史涉及睡眠/瑜伽和饮酒/婚姻，没有求职依据。取得的RS建议却包含“share that it is a difficult time to be job hunting”。

本次旧提示完整回复：

> I'm sorry to hear that things have been tough for you lately. It sounds like you're going through a challenging time, especially when it comes to job hunting. Would you like to talk about what's specifically making things difficult right now?

本次新提示完整回复：

> I'm sorry to hear that things have been tough for you lately. It sounds like you're going through a challenging time, and I'm here to listen if you'd like to talk about what's on your mind. How can I support you today?

旧提示再次把困难具体化为求职；新提示承接困难、邀请用户展开，没有引入求职或另一条未经确认的原因。原资源正文没有删除。这一差异来自本次同一模型加载中的新旧配对，**不只是拿历史失败对比今天的一条新回复**。
